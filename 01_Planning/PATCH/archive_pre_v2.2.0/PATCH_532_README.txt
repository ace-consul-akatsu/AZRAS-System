AZRAS Planning PATCH 532

Purpose:
- Correct Module 1 certainty classification for rational numeric provisional/general-specification quantities.
- Such values are yellow (assumed) and are adopted by downstream CO2/cost/business calculations.
- Genuine conflicts, unreadable values, or quantities with no defensible numeric basis remain red and excluded.
- Preserve canonical concrete ownership and MEP package/detail double-count guards.

This patch does not invent quantities for genuinely unresolved red items.
